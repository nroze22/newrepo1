# 💰 AI Consultant Platform - Business Plan

## Executive Summary

**Problem:** Businesses and individuals pay $1000-$6000+/hour for expert consultants across various domains (business strategy, legal, design, code review, etc.).

**Solution:** AI-powered consulting platform using Gemini's multimodal capabilities to provide expert-level analysis during live video sessions at a fraction of the cost.

**Market:** $250B+ global consulting market + $50B+ SaaS market

**Revenue Model:** Subscription + pay-per-session + usage-based pricing

**Target:** 99%+ gross margins, scalable to millions in revenue

---

## 🎯 Value Proposition

### For Customers:
- **90-95% cost savings** vs traditional consultants
- **Instant availability** - no scheduling delays
- **Multiple expertise areas** in one platform
- **Session recordings + reports** included
- **Privacy** - no human consultant needed for sensitive matters

### Competitive Advantage:
1. **First-mover** in AI-powered video consulting
2. **Multimodal AI** - analyzes video, audio, screen shares simultaneously
3. **7 specialized modes** - business, design, code, legal, medical, real estate, marketing
4. **Real-time analysis** - insights during the session, not after
5. **Professional quality** - trained to provide $5000+/hour level insights
