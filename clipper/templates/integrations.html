Postiz (scheduled publishing)

Publishing is delegated to Postiz, a mature open-source social media scheduler. Postiz handles OAuth per platform, rate limits, retries, the calendar UI, and per-post analytics. We hand it the rendered clip plus the per-platform captions our Packager agent wrote.

Self-host Postiz (docker compose up), connect your channels in its UI, then paste its URL and an API key from Settings → Developers → Public API below.
